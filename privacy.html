Privacy Policy

Bites · last updated 28 October 2026

Bites is a personal project used by a small group of friends and family to log restaurant ratings. This policy describes what it stores and why. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What is stored

  • Your account: email address, username and display name.
  • Your ratings: restaurants, dates, dishes and scores you enter, and the people you tag as fellow diners.
  • Your friends: the accounts you follow, and who follows you.
  • Google Calendar connection (optional): access and refresh tokens, stored encrypted.

Google Calendar data

Bites requests two permissions and uses them for one purpose: finding a dinner time everyone is free for.

  • Free/busy access (calendar.freebusy): the app sees only whether you are busy during a period. It does not receive, request or store event titles, descriptions, guests, locations or attachments.
  • Event creation (calendar.events): when you confirm a proposed dinner, the app creates that one event and invites the people you selected. It does not read, edit or delete any other event.

Free/busy results are used to calculate available times and are not retained after the proposal is generated. Bites' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never transferred to anyone except as needed to create the event you confirmed.

Where it is stored

Data is held in a private Supabase (PostgreSQL) database controlled by the developer. Access is restricted per user by database-level security rules. Google tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are written, using a key held only on the server.

What other people can see

  • Friends you have accepted can see your restaurant ratings.
  • A rating you tag someone on appears in that person's collection, under your name.
  • If you generate a share link, anyone holding that link can view your ratings read-only. You can reset the link at any time, which immediately breaks the old one.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect your calendar in the app, and revoke the app entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.
  • To delete your account and all associated ratings, email the address below and it will be removed.
