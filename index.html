Bites

Log and rate the restaurants you try, and see what your friends thought.

What it does

  • Rate a meal by the dishes you ordered, plus pricing, service and atmosphere — the overall score is calculated for you 8 ⭐
  • Tag the people you ate with, so the meal shows up in their ratings too
  • Follow friends and see their scores for every restaurant in a city
  • Share a read-only link to your collection, or export a city as a PDF
  • Plan a shared meal: the app checks everyone's calendar availability and sends invites for a time that works

Google Calendar

Planning a shared meal is optional, and only works if you connect a Google Calendar. When you do, Bites reads free/busy times only — when you are busy, never the titles, guests, locations or any other detail of your events — and creates the dinner event once you confirm it.

See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and how to revoke access.

Status

A personal project, built for a small group of friends and family. It is not a commercial product and is not open for public sign-ups.
